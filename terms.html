Terms of Service

MechGo Terms of Service

Effective Date: April 4, 2026

Welcome to MechGo ("the App"), a platform that connects customers with independent mechanics. By using MechGo, you agree to these Terms of Service ("Terms"). If you do not agree, do not use the App.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using the App, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to these Terms, our Privacy Policy, and all other applicable policies. These Terms govern your access and use of the App, services, and content.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old. Mechanics must have valid licenses and insurance to provide services. Users must provide accurate information when registering.

3. Services

MechGo provides a platform only. Mechanics are independent contractors; MechGo does not provide repair services directly. All agreements, payments, and interactions between customers and mechanics are between those parties unless otherwise stated.

4. Accounts

Users are responsible for their login credentials and all activity under their account. Account termination may occur for policy violations.

5. Payments

Payments are processed through MechGo's secure system. MechGo charges a service fee or commission. Refunds are handled according to the Refund Policy.

6. User Conduct

Users must not engage in illegal activity, harassment, or fraudulent behavior. Mechanics and customers must act professionally and safely.

7. Liability

MechGo is not responsible for damages, losses, or injuries arising from services provided by mechanics. Services are "as is"; MechGo disclaims warranties to the maximum extent permitted by law.

8. Termination

MechGo may suspend or terminate accounts at any time for violations. Users may deactivate accounts voluntarily.

9. Dispute Resolution

Users agree to attempt informal resolution first. Unresolved disputes may go to binding arbitration under the laws of the applicable launch jurisdiction.

Privacy Policy

MechGo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 4, 2026

MechGo respects your privacy and protects your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share data.

1. Information Collection

Personal Data: name, phone number, email, and payment information. Location Data: real-time GPS location for service delivery. Device Data: device type, operating system, and usage patterns.

2. How We Use Data

We use data to facilitate service requests and payments, improve app performance and user experience, send notifications regarding jobs and promotions, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing Data

Relevant information may be shared with mechanics for service delivery, with service providers for payment processing, and with law enforcement or legal authorities when required.

4. Data Security

Sensitive data should be encrypted and stored securely, with regular backups and secure API connections such as HTTPS in a production launch.

5. User Rights

Users may access, update, or delete personal information and opt out of marketing communications, subject to platform and legal requirements.

Refunds and Cancellation

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Customers may cancel a request before a mechanic accepts it without penalty. Once a mechanic accepts the request, cancellations may be subject to fees, generally in the 10% to 20% range of the service.

Refunds are processed through the same payment method used for the booking and may take approximately 5 to 10 business days depending on the payment provider.

Disputes about services, cancellations, billing, or refund eligibility are handled through MechGo support and may require review of booking records, communications, GPS matching, or other platform data.

Mechanic Agreement

Mechanic Agreement / Contractor Terms

Mechanics using MechGo are independent contractors, not employees. Mechanics must provide valid licenses, insurance, and identification and remain responsible for the quality and safety of their work.

Mechanics agree to applicable MechGo service fees or commissions and must maintain required insurance coverage while following safety guidelines, dispatch rules, and any applicable local requirements.

Safety and Liability

Safety & Liability Disclaimer

MechGo does not guarantee the quality or outcome of any mechanic service. Users are encouraged to verify mechanic credentials before authorizing work.

Mechanics should ensure all safety precautions are followed during roadside work, especially in traffic, severe weather, low-visibility, or high-risk repair situations.

MechGo is not liable for accidents, vehicle damage, personal injury, or other losses resulting from services performed by independent mechanics using the platform.
